Get the app

phone

  1. Install Tailscale and sign in

    Use the same account as your server. Tailscale on Google Play

  2. Install Telemetry

    With Obtainium (recommended): it installs the app from GitHub and keeps it updated. Obtainium asks you to confirm, then shows an install button.

    open in Obtainium get Obtainium first

    Or download the APK and open it. Android asks you once to allow installs from your browser. You won't get updates this way.

    download telemetry-phone.apk

  3. Connect it

    In the panel on your computer, open Settings → Devices → Add a device and scan its QR with this phone's camera: the app opens with everything filled in and connects. Or type your server's https://… address and the setup code into the app. Then grant the permissions it walks you through.
